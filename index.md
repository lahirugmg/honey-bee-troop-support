# Honey Bee Troop — Support

Honey Bee Troop is a local-first meeting notes app for Mac. It lives in your
menu bar, detects meetings automatically, transcribes them on-device, and
turns the conversation into structured notes.

## Contact

Questions, bug reports, or feature requests: **lahirugmg@gmail.com**

## Frequently asked questions

**Does Honey Bee Troop require an account?**
No. There's no sign-in, no account, and no subscription to use the core
features. AI processing runs on-device by default.

**Does audio or transcript data leave my Mac?**
Not by default. Speech recognition and diarization run entirely on-device.
Notes and summaries are generated by a small open-source model that runs
locally on your Mac's Neural Engine. Data only leaves your device if you
choose to connect an optional AI provider (Ollama, OpenAI, or Claude) in
Settings — that's off by default and always your choice.

**Why does the app ask for Screen Recording permission?**
Screen Recording is requested solely to capture *system audio* — the other
side of a call — via ScreenCaptureKit, which is the only sandbox-legal way to
do that on macOS. No video or screen content is ever captured, recorded, or
stored. If you decline, the app falls back to microphone-only recording.

**How do I turn off automatic meeting recording?**
Settings → General → Meeting Detection → "Record detected meetings
automatically." You're always responsible for getting consent from other
participants before recording a call.

**Where are my notes and recordings stored?**
Locally on your Mac, inside the app's sandboxed container. Nothing is
uploaded unless you explicitly connect a cloud AI provider.

**I found a bug or have a feature request.**
Email lahirugmg@gmail.com with a description of what happened and, if
possible, steps to reproduce it.
